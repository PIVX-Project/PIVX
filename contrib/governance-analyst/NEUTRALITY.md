# Neutrality and corrections — draft

This is a proposed independent public research project. It is not an official PIVX or pivx.ai service. Reports help readers inspect proposals and evidence; they do not recommend yes/no votes, copy votes, or operate wallets or masternodes.

Michael Korovkin is the intended Phase 1 human reviewer. His PIVX roles, funding and relationships with proposers have not yet been supplied. Disclosure status is **pending**. Do not interpret that as a declaration of no affiliations. Publication is blocked until he confirms a disclosure in the report and reviews this policy. Conflicts should be named and, where possible, addressed through an additional reviewer; report any remaining limitation.

Use only public proposal data, public forum posts, public repositories/releases and public price sources. Do not request private dashboards, Grafana, shell access, Discord data, invoices behind authentication, or internal communications even when a proposer offers them. State that such claims were not independently checked. Phase 1 invokes no paid model API, hosting, domain purchase or transactions.

Machine-generated material remains a draft until Michael reviews every proposal and approves the exact package. Review records bind the report, rubric, this policy and proposer-history file. Changing any of them invalidates prior approvals. Project scripts cannot create human sign-off; a human must author review records. Local preview is explicitly marked as unreviewed and excluded from publication artifacts.

The proposed correction process is: prepare a review package; after Michael authorizes outreach, post a public invitation linking to the drafts and a public forum reply or repository issue; record the channel URL and start time; allow at least 48 hours for replies; record responses and edits; obtain final human sign-off; then prepare publication. Elapsed time is not evidence that anyone was contacted. The gate requires a real public invitation URL. No invitation has been posted yet.

Publish a dated correction log, preserve the prior JSON, identify changed factual claims and notify affected proposers through an authorized public channel. Track factual corrections separately from changes in judgment or new evidence. The target of at most one factual correction per cycle is a quality metric, not a reason to suppress corrections. Provide a right of reply; do not infer intent from missing records or community disagreements.

Public engagement and maintainer adoption are external outcomes. Do not count our own drafts, invitations or automated activity as community responses. Voting comparisons are observational and cannot establish that these reports changed voting behavior.
